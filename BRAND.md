# Avenor

A fictional architecture and interiors studio identity applied to the existing
Vistal Next.js project. Layouts, scroll timelines, media and animation selectors
are preserved. Internal `vistal` names are implementation namespaces only.

## Palette

- Graphite: #202724
- Chalk: #F7F8F6
- Clay: #E7B49F
- Mist: #E5EBE7
- Secondary text: #5C6861

Content and metadata: `src/data/siteConfig.ts`. Colour tokens:
`src/styles/theme.css`. Brand wordmarks: `src/styles/brand.css`.

Projects, team profiles, journal titles and figures are illustrative demo
content. Existing reference photography is retained; it is not evidence of
commissioned or constructed Avenor projects. `hello@avenor.example` is a reserved
placeholder. Forms are design-only. Search indexing remains disabled.

## Generated Logo

Built-in ImageGen output: `public/brand/avenor-mark.png`.

Prompt: One refined architectural A emblem for AVENOR. Two load-bearing piers,
a negative-space lintel and an open sculptural arch. Quiet editorial identity,
balanced silhouette, legible at 28px. Graphite #202724 with a clay #E7B49F planar
accent. One centred compact emblem, transparent background, no lettering,
mockups, variations, shadows, gradients or textures. The mark is paired with
editable live text in `BrandLogo.tsx`.
